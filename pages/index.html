Deep Sea Crew

A cooperative deep-sea trick-taking game.

Play now · installable

🌐 Peer-to-peer version

No server, no setup. Add it to your home screen and play with a friend over a direct connection (scan a QR to link up). Best for 2 players; works for up to 5.

 Same Wi-Fi · up to 5

📶 LAN version

One device runs a host server; everyone joins from a browser on the same Wi-Fi. Smoothest for 3–5 players — needs a computer (or Android phone) to host.
